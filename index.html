How to Play SolveDoku

  1. The goal is to fill the 9x9 grid so that each row, column, and 3x3 box contains the numbers 1 to 9 exactly once.
  2. Click on a cell and use the keypad or your keyboard to enter a number.
  3. Use the Hint button for help (limited to 5 per puzzle).
  4. Click New Puzzle to start a new game with your chosen difficulty.
  5. Use Solution to reveal the answer, or Reset to clear the board.
  6. Errors are highlighted automatically. Try to avoid duplicates!
  7. Track your time and aim for your best score!

Tip: Use logic, not guessing! Every puzzle has a unique solution.
